It is important to note that democratic participation plays a crucial role in the development of modern political systems. Overall, public trust is a significant factor because citizens need to believe that institutions are fair, transparent, and responsive. Smith (2021) found that participation increased by 12% when local councils published decision timelines online. Furthermore, this shows that digital transparency can have a significant impact on accountability, civic engagement, and democratic legitimacy. In conclusion, the issue is important because participation, transparency, and accountability are important for improving political outcomes.
